1. Who we are and what this policy covers

Marshy Merge is provided by Hammad Mukhtiar, an independent developer based in Pakistan ("we", "us", or "our"). This policy explains how information is handled when you play Marshy Merge, use Floof Climb and the town-building features, visit this policy page, or contact us.

This policy describes the current game and its optional rewarded-video integration. Advertising may be disabled in pre-release or other builds. Online player accounts, cloud saves, score uploads to online leaderboards, and real-money purchases or subscriptions are not active in the current version. We will update this policy before introducing features that change how personal information is handled.

2. Information we handle and why

Game information stored on your device

The game stores progress such as levels, coins, unlocked characters, town layouts, inventory, rewards, tutorial completion, and Floof Climb scores, records, and unfinished runs. It also stores preferences such as music, sound, and accessibility settings. This information supports gameplay, remembers your choices, and lets you resume your progress.

Where the age-selection feature is enabled, the game stores your selected age range and whether you have answered. It does not ask for your exact date of birth. The selection is used to control access to advertising. Consent tools may also store your privacy choices and related consent information.

Information you send to us

If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your email address or telephone number, your message, and any screenshots or device details you send. We use this information to respond, investigate problems, and handle privacy requests. Please avoid sending passwords, payment-card details, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

Device permissions and analytics

The current game does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or precise GPS location. It does not include a separate developer-operated analytics service. Advertising services can process technical and interaction information as explained below.

3. Optional rewarded videos and Google services

When enabled for eligible adult profiles, Marshy Merge uses Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK to offer optional rewarded videos. For example, completing a video can unlock a rescue that lets you continue a Floof Climb run. You can decline the video and finish the run instead. Ad availability and reward eligibility depend on the video service and its completion callback.

Google's advertising SDK collects and shares technical information to deliver and measure advertising, diagnose performance, and detect fraud. Depending on the SDK configuration, consent, and device settings, this includes:

  • An IP address, which can indicate approximate location.
  • App and advertising interactions, such as launches, taps, and video views.
  • Diagnostics about app and SDK performance.
  • Device and account-related identifiers, including advertising and app-set identifiers where available.

The current rewarded-video integration requests non-personalized ads with a general-audience content rating. Non-personalized ads can still involve data processing for delivery, measurement, security, and fraud prevention. They do not mean that no information is collected.

Where required, Google's User Messaging Platform presents privacy choices before advertising is initialized or requested. The game checks the consent service's result before proceeding. Eligible profiles can revisit available choices through Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy options. Declining consent does not block the base game; advertising availability may change.

For details about Google's processing and controls, see Google's Privacy Policy, How Google uses information for advertising, and Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosures. Google's processing is also governed by its own policies.

4. Children and younger players

Marshy Merge includes gameplay that younger players can enjoy. The current age-gated advertising flow is restricted to profiles that select 18 or older. It does not request ads for profiles identified as under 18 or whose age range is unknown. Choosing a younger age range or declining to provide one does not prevent local gameplay.

The game does not require children to create accounts or provide names, email addresses, telephone numbers, or exact dates of birth to play. We do not knowingly use children's personal information for targeted advertising. A parent or guardian who believes a child has sent us personal information can contact us to request review and deletion, subject to applicable legal requirements.

5. Sharing, service providers, and international processing

We do not upload your locally saved game progress to a developer-operated server in the current version. Google processes information when its consent or advertising services are used. Our email or telephone service providers process communications when you contact us. GitHub processes website requests when you visit this page.

We may disclose information we hold if required by applicable law or a valid legal request, or where reasonably necessary to investigate fraud, protect safety, or defend legal rights. We do not sell your local game saves.

We are based in Pakistan. Service providers may process information in other countries, under their published privacy terms and applicable transfer safeguards. See the linked provider policies for further information.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Game progress: retained in the app's local storage until you reset it or remove the relevant app data. Use Settings → Delete Progress to clear game progress, including levels, coins, town data, collections, and Floof Climb records. This action preserves preferences and privacy choices.
  • Other local settings: use Android's app-storage controls to clear the app's data, including stored preferences and age-range selection. This also removes progress. Device or operating-system backups, if present, are controlled separately through the relevant platform.
  • Support messages: retained while needed to resolve your request and for related security, dispute-resolution, or legal obligations. You can ask us to delete information you have sent; any legally required retention will be explained where applicable.
  • Google and other provider data: retained according to the relevant provider's policies and your available controls. Resetting game progress does not delete information already held by Google or other service providers.

We cannot remotely retrieve or erase a game save that exists only on your device. There is no game account to delete in the current version.

7. Your privacy choices and rights

You can choose not to watch rewarded videos, use available ad-privacy controls, manage your advertising identifier through Android settings, and clear locally stored app data. If you withdraw consent, this does not change the lawfulness of processing that occurred before withdrawal.

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or obtain a copy of personal information, or to opt out of certain uses. Contact us using the details below. We may request proportionate information to verify a request, without asking for unnecessary sensitive details. You may also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority.

Where consent is required for advertising-related processing, we use the applicable consent tools. Handling support requests is necessary to respond to you; security and legal processing may rely on legitimate interests or legal obligations where applicable.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information within our control and limit unnecessary data collection. Local saves rely on your device's app-storage protections. Google states that data collected by its advertising SDK is encrypted in transit. No device, network, or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

9. This privacy-policy website and external links

This page is hosted using GitHub Pages. We have not added advertising, analytics scripts, forms, or tracking cookies to this page. GitHub may process technical request information, including IP addresses, to provide and secure its hosting service. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Following an external link takes you to another provider's service, whose own privacy practices apply.
